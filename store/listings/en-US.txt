Title: Universal Tuner
Short title: Tuner

Short description (80 characters max):
Tuner for 34 instruments, metronome and chords. Works fully offline.

Full description:

A tuner that does one thing and gets it right: telling you how far off your
string is, the instant you pluck it.

ACTUALLY WORKS OFFLINE
The app does not request internet permission. There is no way for it to send
anything anywhere, because there is no way for it to send anything. Which also
means it works at the show, in the middle of nowhere, and anywhere the signal
does not reach.

34 INSTRUMENTS
Guitar, 7- and 8-string, 12-string, bass (4, 5 and 6 strings), ukulele,
cavaquinho, violin, viola, cello, banjo, mandolin, mandola, sitar, lute,
balalaika, Portuguese guitar, lap steel and more.
Winds and percussion in chromatic mode, which detects any note automatically.

NON-STANDARD TUNINGS, NO FUSS
Drop D, Drop C, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, baritone, half step down, whole step
down. Switching is one tap — and the dial retargets to the right note
immediately.

CAPO SIMULATOR
Pick the fret and the app computes the effective tuning. With a capo on the
3rd fret your guitar is G major, and that is the G it tunes to — the note you
actually hear.

ADJUSTABLE REFERENCE
A4 from 415 to 466 Hz, for anyone tuning at 432 or 442. Plus a fine adjustment
in cents, so you tune to your own guitar and not to the factory one.

A METRONOME THAT STAYS IN TIME
The beat comes from the device's audio clock, not from the system. Measured:
10 microseconds of drift after 10 minutes. No speeding up along with the error.

TOOLS
• Note card with frequencies, for tuning by ear
• Chords and fretboard, respecting your tuning and capo
• Idea recorder, so the phrase you just found is not lost
• Favourites with export and import

BUILT FOR ONE HAND
Large targets, a screen that does not sleep mid-tuning, and it works well with
the instrument resting against your body. Includes spoken readout for screen
reader users.

What this app does not do: create an account, show ads, or request any
permission beyond the microphone.
